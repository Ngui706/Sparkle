Which areas do you serve?

We serve Karatina University hostels, staff residences, Karatina town and nearby estates. Contact us to confirm pickup from your specific location.

Our standard turnaround is 48 hours. Same-day service is available for selected orders booked early.

We accept M-Pesa, card and cash on delivery. We’ll share payment details when your order is confirmed.
